## Issue
High rate of layover in Amazon will affect the entire operation in Amazon Fulfillment center and HR manager , by reducing the efficiency and consistent effort to hire candidates. This issue of high layover should be resolved as soon as possible to enhance the productivity in Amazon Fulfillment and reputation of Amazon work environment

## Background
According to Glassdoor. Amazon Fulfillment center has a rating of 3.2 of 5. Reviews between year of 2024 and 2025 shown that 18 out of 100 workers mentioned exhausting conditions(like consistent lifting heavy items), and poor management, long hours, time constraints, and problematic point systems

## Analysis
Based on background study, poor management and intense physical strength are the 2 main factors that caused layovers in Amazon fulfillment centres . The reason for this is because of the fast paced environment that Amazon was in to always deliver packages to customers as quickly as possible , forcing workers to be quick and precise. Given that this is an ongoing issue in many logistic companies like Target, Sam’s Club, the challenge of high layover is yet not been resolved by any big companies.
